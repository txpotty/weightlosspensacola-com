    Rapid dissolve tablets are taken as needed, usually 15 to 60 minutes before sex depending on the formula.     Side Effects in Pensacola

    Side effects follow the active ingredient. Headache, flushing, stuffy nose, indigestion and dizziness are common with both sildenafil and tadalafil formulas, and tadalafil versions can also cause back or muscle aches the next day. The base can irritate the mouth slightly. Seek emergency care for an erection over four hours or sudden vision or hearing changes. Rare, but serious.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule these tablets out, including nitroglycerin, isosorbide and poppers. Riociguat is also excluded. Recent heart attack or stroke, chest pain at rest, poorly controlled blood pressure and serious liver or kidney disease call for a close look. Alpha blockers like tamsulosin and drugs like ketoconazole or ritonavir raise drug levels. Food allergies get asked about too.

    Rapid Dissolve Tablets Compared to Other Options in Pensacola

    Compounded rapid dissolve tablets are not FDA-approved. Generic sildenafil and tadalafil tablets contain the same active ingredients, are FDA-approved and have far more research behind them, which is why providers often start there. Troches take longer to dissolve. A troche sits between the cheek and gum for 10 to 20 minutes. Daily chewables offer steady low-dose coverage instead.
