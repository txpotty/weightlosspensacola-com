    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Pensacola, FL

    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Pensacola

    Most modern weight loss shots are GLP-1 medicines. They copy a hormone your gut releases after eating, which slows stomach emptying, signals fullness to the brain and helps regulate blood sugar after a meal. Semaglutide targets one receptor. Tirzepatide acts on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, which may explain its larger average effect in trials.

    Who Weight Loss Injections Are For

    FDA-approved injections are for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. A BMI of 27 or above counts too when paired with a condition tied to weight, like type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, sleep apnea or high cholesterol. They work alongside diet and exercise. Pregnancy and some thyroid cancer histories rule them out.

    Injection Dosing Schedule in Pensacola

    Every weekly injection starts low. Wegovy begins at 0.25 mg and climbs every four weeks through 0.5, 1 and 1.7 mg to a maintenance dose of 2.4 mg, though some people stay at 1.7 mg. Zepbound starts at 2.5 mg and rises in 2.5 mg steps to as high as 15 mg. Same day each week.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Appetite often drops in the first week. The first month tends to show small changes on the scale since the dose is low, while stomach upset clusters right after each shot. In the STEP 1 trial, adults on semaglutide 2.4 mg lost about 15 percent of body weight on average over 68 weeks, and SURMOUNT-1 reported about 20 percent with tirzepatide 15 mg over 72 weeks. Results vary.

    Injection Side Effects in Pensacola

    Nausea is the classic one. Diarrhea, constipation, vomiting, heartburn and mild redness at the injection site are also common, especially in the days after starting or raising the dose. Eating slowly and skipping greasy food on shot day takes the edge off. Less common but serious problems include pancreatitis, gallstones and kidney strain from dehydration.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with BMI and medical history. Your provider then reviews every medication you take, looking hardest at insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills, since tirzepatide can make oral contraceptives less reliable after dose changes. Medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 in your family rules out GLP-1 injections. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are too.

    Brand-Name vs Compounded Injections in Pensacola

    Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for weight management. Ozempic and Mounjaro share those ingredients but carry type 2 diabetes labels instead. Compounded versions of semaglutide and tirzepatide come from licensed compounding pharmacies and lack FDA approval, which means no FDA review of their safety, effectiveness or manufacturing. Some patients choose them for availability.
